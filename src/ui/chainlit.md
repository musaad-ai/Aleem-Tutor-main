# أهلاً بك في عليم  ·  Welcome to Aleem

**Aleem** ("knowledgeable" in Arabic) is a curriculum-grounded AI tutor for
Saudi school students. Every answer comes from your official Ministry of
Education textbook — no foreign curriculum, no hallucinations.

---

## How to use

1. **Pick your grade** from the cards on the welcome screen.
2. **Pick your subject** from the settings panel (⚙ gear icon at the top).
3. **Ask your question** in Arabic or English. Aleem will answer using only
   your selected grade's textbook for that subject, with inline `[n]`
   citations pointing to the exact lesson and page.

## Refusal as a feature

If the answer isn't in your textbook, Aleem will tell you so — and surface
related lessons from your grade. It will **never** invent an answer.
